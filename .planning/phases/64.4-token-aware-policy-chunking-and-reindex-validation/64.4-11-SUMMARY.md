## Decisions Made

- Commit the bundle with one atomic manifest-directory rename only after all four immutable payload files are staged, fsynced, and published.
- Treat a missing manifest as unreadable partial state, accept only byte-identical retries, and reject any conflicting bytes.
- Keep diagnostic generation outside all selection, activation, pointer, and history authority paths.
- Mark rollback as proved only after a newly opened session reproduces the exact baseline after cleanup.
