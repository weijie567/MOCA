## Decisions Made

- Current production methods accept tenant/logical keys only; caller-selected corpus IDs remain limited to explicitly named exact evaluation/reindex DTOs, and history accepts retained immutable identity instead.
- Generation name, config schema, and canonical fingerprint must agree before choosing an assembler; mode flags cannot override production active state.
- Canonical document v2 remains derived from ordered source blocks before chunking. Corpus visibility binds compatible immutable rows but does not participate in document/chunk/evidence identity.
- Append-only policy projections cannot be safely reset or synthesized by the old demo shortcut. The script therefore requires an already ingested active corpus and refuses destructive reset; Plan 08 owns ongoing source-manifest/delete continuity.
