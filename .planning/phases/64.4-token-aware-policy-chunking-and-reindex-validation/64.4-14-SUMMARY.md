## Decisions Made

- Canonicality is a production-entry property rooted in the repository, not a caller-selected output directory. Unit artifact APIs retain temporary-root injection solely for deterministic tests.
- The manifest binds actual state and descriptor file-byte hashes. Schema payload self-hashes are not substituted for file-byte authority.
- Recovery authorization remains separate from immutable v1 run/selection bytes, avoiding retroactive schema mutation while adding a strict machine-verifiable lineage.
- Rollback-to-prior continues to require no selection proof. Real selected cutover and restore-selected require verified authorization; Plan08 fixtures retain their deliberately separate type and schema.
