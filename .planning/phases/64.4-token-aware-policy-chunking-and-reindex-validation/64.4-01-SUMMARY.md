## Decisions Made

- The vendored asset is official Qwen source material, but its mapping to DashScope `text-embedding-v4` is labeled `empirically_provider_parity_approved_not_vendor_guaranteed`; no code or document upgrades that evidence into a provider promise.
- The full configuration JSON is canonicalized with UTF-8, sorted keys, and compact separators. Ambient paths and live parity state are excluded from the fingerprint.
- `EmbeddingTokenCounter.count()` always includes the asset-defined EOS and performs two encodes; it never accepts a character count, download, alternate tokenizer, or best-effort fallback.
- Live parity remains a separate downstream authorization input. `unavailable` and `quarantined` raise typed failures; only `passed` can unblock a later caller, and this plan does not perform provider calls or persistence.
