## Decisions Made

- The Plan 01 `EmbeddingTokenCounter` is the only token authority. No tokenizer asset/runtime is loaded here, and no character length becomes a hidden budget estimate or failure fallback.
- The exact provider envelope uses title/section plus sorted unique `source_block_id` lines. The returned immutable string is the only value later plans may submit; ingestion/embedder convergence remains Plan 04 scope.
- Structural split pieces retain explicit source joiners: a parser-block boundary contributes a newline, while pieces of the same source block concatenate without inserted bytes.
- Overlap copies only a suffix of the preceding primary citation body in the same section. Its text and complete next provider input are both recounted; source refs expand only when non-empty overlap is retained.
- Empty/whitespace-only inputs terminate with no chunks; envelope-dominant input, tokenizer error, non-progress, or final overflow fails closed before any embedding or persistence side effect.
