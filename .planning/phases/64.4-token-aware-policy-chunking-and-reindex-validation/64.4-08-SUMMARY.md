## Decisions Made

- Kept exactly one rollout row per tenant as the sole authority. A corpus stays `complete` through cutover/rollback/restore; activation is not encoded by rewriting lifecycle state.
- Chose append-only same-config COW for ordinary ingestion because the existing append-only triggers, FK RESTRICT relationships and unique `PolicyDocument` head cannot safely support destructive replacement.
- Recognized assemblers exclusively from pinned `config_schema_version + config_fingerprint`; `generation_name` remains descriptive and may carry `:ingest:<manifest_revision>`.
- Refused rollback/restore of any corpus marked `source_stale`; operators rebuild current source under the desired prior config instead.
- Kept Plan 08 fixture-only: no provider call, real A/B selection artifact, selection-success claim, receipt hash chain or report semantics were introduced.
