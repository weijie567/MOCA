## Decisions Made

- Descriptor and budget timestamps derive from sealed authority; recovery never recomputes UUIDs, leases, config, parity, or source identity.
- Missing result after a reserved ordinal is a consumed crash, not implicit permission to retry. Only a committed allowlisted provider-unavailable/transient result permits the second ordinal.
- A committed document advance is success authority: `recover-state` may publish the missing post-state and hash-only success result, but cannot rerun the document.
- Reviewed build request counts are fixed before provider access from assembled input count and sealed provider batch size.
