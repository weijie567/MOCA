|----------|-------------|
| recommendation/claim state -> risk_gate | Actionable LLM output enters deterministic risk and binding validation. |
| risk_gate -> approval_gate/action_draft | Route authority crosses from graph state into approval or draft creation. |

| T-34-02-02 | Tampering | `assess_risk_and_approval` | mitigate | Build `RiskDecisionV1` and target merchant binding from service-approved business facts and verified claim/evidence refs only. |
| T-34-02-03 | Spoofing | `approval_gate` responsibility boundary | mitigate | Static tests prove approval_gate does not re-decide blocked/approval-required/auto-draft policy. |
</threat_model>
