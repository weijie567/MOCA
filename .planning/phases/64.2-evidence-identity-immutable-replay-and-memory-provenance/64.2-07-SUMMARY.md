## Decisions Made

- Legacy migration uses a fail-closed proof standard. Existing hashes or refs are preserved literally inside the unresolved envelope but are never relabeled canonical merely because their shape looks plausible.
- A source authority entry must be successful, match its business-fact or policy-evidence kind, and carry the matching complete typed ref. Contextual-only and unknown observations cannot become source authority.
- The CaseMemory envelope itself remains `contextual_only` before and after human review. Review only adds exact decision, reviewer, time, and reason while incrementing lifecycle version.
- Public detail and action responses are constructed from typed bounded fields. Stored `provenance_json`, embeddings, raw tool/CWC bodies, rejected observations, and internal mismatch reasons are never returned.
- The request accepts `expected_lifecycle_version` and responses expose current lifecycle version, but this plan intentionally does not implement Plan 08 CAS, expiry, claim, or concurrency behavior.
