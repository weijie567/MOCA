## Decisions Made

- Kept the accepted MVP scope literal and closed: `tenant_policy` plus the serialized tenant UUID. Merchant/global/custom policy scope cannot be inferred or added without a new versioned identity contract.
- Preserved `EvidenceRefV1.build()` as legacy/unbound behavior until Plan 02 changes the writer boundary. This avoids claiming canonical provenance from mutable or caller-supplied values.
- Made migration 025 strictly additive. Only the inactive rollout singleton is seeded; no mutable head receives a sequence and no immutable version is inferred during upgrade.
- Enforced retained history at the database boundary so application bugs cannot rewrite canonical identity material or purge referenced evidence during audit retention.
