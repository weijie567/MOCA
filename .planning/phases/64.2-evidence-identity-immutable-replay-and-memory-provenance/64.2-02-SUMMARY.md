## Decisions Made

- Kept rollout epochs stable across ordinary writes; epoch increments are reserved for control-plane transitions, while the database-native ingestion sequence orders every successful data-plane write.
- Required exact `scope_type="tenant_policy"` and `scope_id=str(tenant_id)` at storage, current minting, historical resolution, and legacy upgrade. Merchant context remains an eligibility filter and never becomes evidence identity.
- Made canonical current retrieval a production session-backed capability. If rollout reads are disabled, quarantined, stale, or missing an exact binding, retrieval returns no refs and never reconstructs a mutable alias.
- Preserved ranking, effective-date filtering, and the existing `EvidenceRefV1` envelope; the cutover changes identity provenance, not retrieval-quality behavior.
