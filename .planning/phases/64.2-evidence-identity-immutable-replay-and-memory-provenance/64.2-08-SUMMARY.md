## Decisions Made

- The claim key is exactly `(identity_algorithm_version, tenant_id, scope_type, scope_id, candidate_hash, content_hash, source_identity_hash)`; content-only or source-only matching is not idempotency.
- Terminal claims remain queryable authority forever. A delayed exact submit returns `identity_conflict` with `memory_id=None`; tombstones remain an additional content/source rewrite boundary, not the primary exact claim owner.
- Approval leaves the exact claim active at the next lifecycle version; rejection, pending expiry, correction supersession, deletion, and forgetting terminalize it.
- Correction creates a new approved exact owner with both direct `corrects/supersedes` fields and one normalized `correction` lineage link. An identical retry must prove the persisted corrected payload, reviewer, provenance, lineage, run, reason, and original event before replay.
