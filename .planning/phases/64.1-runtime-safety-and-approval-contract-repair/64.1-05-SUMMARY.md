## Decisions Made

- Canonical durable outcome evidence wins over the deprecated compatibility `action_result` only when every identity, lifecycle, demo, nested-outcome, and audit check passes. Any incomplete evidence remains fail-closed.
- The graph keeps one `final_response` node for rendering, but `terminal_error` is a distinct route key and produces `final_status=error`; routing through the renderer no longer implies success.
- Agent-run and approval callers invoke memory finalization only for completed responses, while the memory service repeats the terminal check to prevent a bad caller status from laundering failure into persistence.
- Internal tool/store/binding details are never copied into SSE or final response; the public terminal uses `ACTION_DRAFT_TERMINAL_FAILED` and one stable Chinese message.
