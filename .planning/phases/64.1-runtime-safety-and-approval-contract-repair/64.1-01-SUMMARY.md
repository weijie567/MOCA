## Decisions Made

- Kept `ActionResolution.executable_action_type` for compatibility while making the serialized `canonical_action` projection the cross-node authority.
- A strict structured candidate accepts only `{"action_type": <string>}`; extra keys or non-string shapes are schema-invalid and fail closed.
- Alias overlaps contained inside a longer registry alias (for example `refund` inside `full refund`) resolve to the specific longer alias, while independent actions in one value are ambiguous.
