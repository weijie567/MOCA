## Decisions Made

- Resolve validated fixture paths under the repository root before the production call, but serialize only Plan 01's repository-relative paths and hashes.
- Treat an injected registry as a contract double by construction. It may test call shape and scoring but cannot claim `parser_direct` or provider-baseline identity.
- Keep parser exception contents out of memory-backed reports: a thrown call becomes `parser_invariant_error` plus `parser_exception_sanitized`, while the original exception is never serialized.
- Keep a completed red parser baseline useful: metric/dimension misses are `completed_quality_fail`; only missing prerequisites are unavailable, and evaluator/parser invariants remain execution errors.
