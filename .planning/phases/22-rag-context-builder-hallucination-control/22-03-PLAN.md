<threat_model>
## Trust Boundaries

| Boundary | Description |
|----------|-------------|
| candidate evidence -> ContextBuilder | Retrieved refs are untrusted until canonical re-fetch validates tenant, scope, duplicate key, hash, freshness/effective-at, and latest/current version. |
| repository rows -> prompt projection | Canonical policy content can contain policy text or OCR-derived text that must be bounded and projected safely. |
| provenance/OCR/source-block internals -> labels | Internal provenance may inform risk labels but must not become authority identity or ordinary prompt/final output. |
| bundle debug trace -> downstream state | Debug traces must remain redacted/bounded and separate from ordinary prompt, memory, replay, business facts, and action snapshots. |

## STRIDE Threat Register

| Threat ID | Severity | Category | Component | Disposition | Mitigation Plan |
|-----------|----------|----------|-----------|-------------|-----------------|
| T-22-01 | high | Information Disclosure / Elevation of Privilege | `ContextBuilder` and `PolicyKnowledgeService` | mitigate/block | Re-fetch through tenant-scoped service, validate tenant/scope, and exclude invalid refs before bundle inclusion; verify with `test_phase22_evidence_validation.py`. |
| T-22-02 | high | Tampering | evidence detail lookup | mitigate/block | Compare canonical content hash and latest/current policy version, reject duplicates and stale refs, and record exclusion reason codes; verify latest/current tests explicitly. |
| T-22-03 | high | Tampering / Information Disclosure | prompt/verifier/final projections | mitigate/block | Use bounded snippets, safe labels, and existing projector/budget patterns; verify raw OCR/provenance/debug sentinels do not appear. |
| T-22-04 | high | Spoofing / Tampering | bundle authority fields | mitigate/block | Accept only trusted runtime context and Tool System refs in builder inputs; never treat user/model-authored refs as authority. |
| T-22-05 | high | Elevation of Privilege | route-dependent bundle status | mitigate/block | Emit typed exclusion/status codes that later deterministic routing consumes; no model-chosen route field is accepted in this plan. |
| T-22-06 | high | Tampering / Elevation of Privilege | action-boundary evidence inputs | mitigate/block | Do not emit action-snapshot evidence from non-validated refs; only current allowlisted evidence can flow to later action-boundary plans. |
| T-22-07 | high | Information Disclosure | debug/verifier projections | mitigate/block | Keep debug/verifier projections redacted and separate; verify leakage tests before downstream integration. |
</threat_model>
