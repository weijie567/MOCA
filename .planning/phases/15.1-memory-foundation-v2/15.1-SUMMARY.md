The implementation keeps the owner split intact:

- Conversation rows record what happened in user/assistant/tool conversation facts and safe refs.
- Tool result rows keep raw refs/hashes, normalized JSON, persisted summaries, and prompt summaries separate.
- Session memory remains the Phase 12 slot-continuity layer.
