> This is a fresh threat-mitigation verdict for the implemented C0 surface in Plans 01–04 only. It does not audit or claim implementation of Plans 05–08, authorize provider dispatch, or complete SC-64.4-5/6.

## Verdict

The implemented C0 surface has **7/7 consolidated threats closed and 0 open threats**. All 16 prose threat-model rows in Plans 01–04 are classified below. The plans contain no `accept` or `transfer` language, so every row has the implicit disposition `mitigate`.

The former open threat, T-64.5-06, is closed at the audited HEAD. One authoritative 37-path provider-execution graph now covers the current promotion/checker boundary, both production dispatch and hard-disable owners, reviewed-build and canonical A/B orchestration, provider/retry code, request enumeration, and their call-graph dependencies. The repository and checker consume that same graph for dirty checks and the canonical C0→C1 diff. Current promotion is rechecked before authority issue, reservation, and dispatch. The parameterized dirty-path test exercises every graph member and proves refusal at attestation, promotion candidate, promotion, issue, reservation, and dispatch-recheck gates before downstream mutation/provider seams.

The C0 production commands remain hard-disabled. This verdict is therefore **SECURED for the bounded C0 implementation only**; it is not permission to enable `build-next-reviewed` or `run-ab`. Plans 05–08 retain ownership of later review/promotion, live execution, activation receipts, and closeout.
