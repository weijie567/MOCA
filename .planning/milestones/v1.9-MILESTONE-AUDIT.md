## Integration Review

The integration checker found **0 critical runtime gaps**.

Confirmed wired flow families:

1. Trusted context flow: API/auth/run boundaries create `TrustedContext`, then project into tool, knowledge, memory, approval, and replay contexts.
2. Target graph flow: `rag_context_build`, `claim_verify`, `approval_gate`, and `action_draft` are registered and routed through RAG, claim verification, risk, approval, action draft, and final response.
3. RAG and claim flow: invalid/unsupported states fail closed; claim verification gates risk/action routing.
4. Tool platform flow: `ToolPlatform` owns visibility/invoke; `ToolRuntime` emits `ToolPolicyDecision`; replay validates `DecisionEventEnvelopeV1`.
5. Business fact flow: graph/tool paths consume `BusinessFactService` / `BusinessFactResultV1`; memory, RAG, LLM inference, and raw rows are not accepted as current business fact authority.
6. Approval/action flow: `risk_gate` owns blocked/approval-required/auto-draft routing; approval handles trusted resume; action drafts bind payload/evidence/claim/risk/safety fields.
7. Replay/eval flow: dev-contract gates are wired; release/monitoring gates exist and remain intentionally non-blocking.
